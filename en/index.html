Frequently asked questions

What is Impact Capital VC?

Impact Capital VC is a venture capital fund that invests in non-standard, unconventional agentic AI companies. It was created by venture capitalists, business leaders, technology entrepreneurs and scientists.

What does Impact Capital VC invest in?

The fund backs agentic AI businesses — companies whose AI agents autonomously perform work such as research, marketing, monitoring and content operations — especially in overlooked markets and cross-border corridors.

Which companies are in the Impact Capital VC portfolio?

The portfolio includes Defy, ToucHigh, Matrex Analytica, SNMRush, Mindfuse and Somonitor.

Founders building unconventional agentic AI companies can reach the team through the contact link on this website.
